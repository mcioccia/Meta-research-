## 2. Furthest Corner Analysis & Prediction

- **Furthest Corner (Upside / Bull case):** **Top-Right corner (8.4% WACC, 3.5% terminal growth) = $369.19 per share** (+$101.06 / +37.7% above the $268.13 base case).
  - *Why it is furthest:* Terminal value accounts for ~78.4% of Enterprise Value in the base case. Compressing the spread $(WACC - g)$ from $6.9\%$ down to $4.9\%$ expands the terminal value capitalization multiple by $+40.8\%$, lifting the terminal value at Year 5 from $\$819.79\text{B}$ to $\$1,164.73\text{B}$.
  - *Market divergence:* Even at this most optimistic corner ($369.19), the value remains far below market pricing (~56% of today's price of $653.69, and ~50% of the $738.31 filing-date price). This proves that discount rate and terminal growth sensitivities alone cannot close the valuation gap.

## 3. Reverse DCF Analysis

Solving for the constant addition ($\Delta$) across all five explicit annual growth rates:

- **At today's price ($653.69):** requires **about +19.53 points on every growth rate**.
  - Implied growth trajectory: **−40.47%, +79.53%, +59.53%, +39.53%, +29.53%**
- **At filing-date price ($738.31):** requires **about +22.53 points on every growth rate**.
  - Implied growth trajectory: **−37.47%, +82.53%, +62.53%, +42.53%, +32.53%**

**Takeaway:** The market is not assuming Meta's planned $115–135B capex causes a severe -60% FCFF contraction with sluggish recovery. Instead, prevailing market prices require Year 1 cash flow to hold up much better (~−37% to −40%) and subsequent growth to compound at ~30%–80% annually as AI investments monetize.
