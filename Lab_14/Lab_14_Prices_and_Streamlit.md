## What happens when someone moves a control?

**By default, Streamlit reruns the entire Python script from top to bottom with the control's updated value.** It recalculates the output and redraws the page; it does not run only the line next to the slider.

If a widget has a callback, the callback runs before the rest of the script. Caching can reuse expensive results. Forms can delay updates until submission, and fragments can limit reruns to part of an app. These are exceptions or additional features; a normal control triggers a full rerun. [Streamlit: Data flow](https://docs.streamlit.io/get-started/fundamentals/main-concepts#data-flow).

For a future portfolio app, keep price downloads outside ordinary reruns or cache them. Changing a weight should recalculate the portfolio from the saved prices rather than download five years of data every time.
